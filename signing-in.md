# Signing in
{: .no_toc }

## What you'll do

Sign in to Paperwork, or accept an invitation to a collection, with an account you already have.

## Before you start

You need a **Microsoft, Google or GitHub** account. Those are the only ways to sign in. Paperwork has no passwords of its own, and no email and password sign-in.

You do not need an account to open a public template in the [Viewer](/paperwork.view/). You need one to create templates in the Designer, and to open templates in a collection.

## Steps

**1. Open the Designer, or the link you were given.**

**2. Choose your account.** Choose Microsoft, Google or GitHub, and sign in with that account as you normally would. Paperwork never sees the password.

Use the account with the email address your organisation invited. If you are not sure which that is, ask whoever invited you.

### Accepting an invitation

**1. Open the email and choose Open your invite.** Use the link in the email, not the Paperwork home page. A personal Microsoft account may only be able to accept through that link.

**2. Sign in and accept.** The invite is accepted in one of two ways:

- If the account you sign in with has the invited email address, and that address is verified by Microsoft, Google or GitHub, the invite is accepted.
- If it does not, the invite link carries a one-time code that accepts the invite instead.

If the email is lost, the invite page has a way to send it again.

## Check it worked

You are signed in and can open what you were invited to. A new member sees nothing in a collection until a Publisher has added templates to it. See [Roles and access](/roles/).

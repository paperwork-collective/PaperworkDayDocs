# Fill in a document and download it
{: .no_toc }

## What you'll do

Open the document you were sent, fill in the fields and save the PDF. About two minutes, and nothing to install.

{: .note }
> **If you set Paperwork up for other people,** send them this page with the link to their document. It is written for the people who fill documents in, not for the people who build them.

## Before you start

- The link to your document, from whoever manages your documents. A public template opens for anyone. A private one asks you to sign in.
- A web browser. A phone works as well as a computer.
- If you are asked to sign in, an account with Microsoft, Google or GitHub. Use the one your organisation invited. If you are not sure which, ask the person who sent the link. See [Signing in](/signing-in/).

## Steps

**1. Open the link.** The document appears on the page, already made from its template.

**2. Sign in if you are asked.** Choose Microsoft, Google or GitHub, and sign in as you normally would. Paperwork never sees your password.

<div class="pw-shot"><span class="pw-shot-id">V14</span><span class="pw-shot-what">The sign-in choices: Microsoft, Google and GitHub.</span><span class="pw-shot-file">assets/images/viewer/v14-sign-in-choices.png</span></div>

**3. Choose Fill in.** The fields for this document open. Each one is a value that changes from one document to the next, such as a name, a date or a choice from a list.

<div class="pw-shot"><span class="pw-shot-id">V2</span><span class="pw-shot-what">The Fill in panel open beside the document, with a text field, a date and a choice filled in.</span><span class="pw-shot-file">assets/images/viewer/v02-fill-in-panel.png</span></div>

**4. Fill them in.** The document shows your values, so you can see the result before you save it. Some fields are required: those need a value, and the others can be left as they are.

<div class="pw-shot"><span class="pw-shot-id">V3</span><span class="pw-shot-what">A document with a picture field and a map field filled in.</span><span class="pw-shot-file">assets/images/viewer/v03-image-and-map-fields.png</span></div>

**5. Check the pages.** Scroll through the document, and use the zoom controls to read the detail. If the side panel is available, it shows the pages as thumbnails and the outline of the document.

**6. Choose Download.** The PDF is saved to your computer, ready to print, attach to an email or file.

## More you can do

If your document has a **More** menu, it can hold some of these:

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy link** | Copies the link to the document. |
| **Open in a new tab** | Opens the document in a tab of its own. |
| **Document details** | Shows the document's title and other details. |

Which of these you see is decided by whoever put the document on the page, so you may see fewer.

## On a phone

The same steps work on a phone. The screen is narrower, so the toolbar and panels are smaller.

## Check it worked

- The document shows your values, not the example values.
- The downloaded PDF opens and matches what you saw on the screen.

## If something is wrong

- **You are asked to sign in and it does not let you in.** Check you are using the account your organisation invited, and that you were added to the collection. Ask whoever sent you the link.
- **A message says the document is not available to you.** You are signed in, but not allowed to open it. Ask whoever sent you the link to add you.
- **The document is blank or a picture is missing.** Reload the page. If it is still missing, tell whoever sent you the link.

More answers for people using a document are on [Paperwork Support](https://paperwork.support).
